Privacy policy — Kevin

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who this is for

Kevin is a private assistant operated by Viktor Nyitray for his own use. The only Google accounts connected to it are accounts Viktor owns.

What data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages to sort them, applies labels, and saves draft replies. Mail is sent only after Viktor approves each message.
  • Google Calendar: reads the calendar list and free/busy times, and creates or edits events Viktor approves.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give Viktor email triage, reply drafts and calendar help. Parts of messages may be processed by AI model providers under API terms that do not allow them to train on the data. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Kevin's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on a private server reachable only over a private network. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request.
